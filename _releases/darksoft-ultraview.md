A 10-song indie rock album with dream pop, jangle and shoegaze sounds, about stepping back to see life from afar. Written, recorded and produced by Darksoft in his home studio in Maine, with additional drums and percussion by Jacob Tessier. Mixed and mastered by Brian Fisher; artwork by Jameson Martin.
