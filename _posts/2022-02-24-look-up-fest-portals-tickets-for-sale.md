SEATTLE, WA. Tickets are now on sale! Look Up Fest returns for its fourth manifestation on Saturday, March 12, at The Rendezvous in Belltown. Join us for a night of nine dreamy bands, trippy visuals, and DIY immersive installations on the theme of Portals. 🌀 Grab your tix at [lufp.eventbrite.com](https://lufp.eventbrite.com).
