Look Up Records is excited to announce that we've signed [**Sealife**](/sealife)**.**Sealife is bringing love-coated psychedelic pop from Seattle straight to your heart. Their sonic storytelling paints a landscape of vulnerable lullabies.

<figure><img src="/assets/img/sealife-promo-4-d986e3.webp" alt="Sealife promo 4" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Sealife: The new psychedelic dreamer in the Look Up family</figcaption></figure>

Originating from bedroom pop and stream-of-consciousness songwriting, Sealife pulls from Sade, Steely Dan, and The Smiths to craft lush sonic dreams. With a nod to Seattle grunge, Sealife is no stranger to fuzz or esoteric lyrics.

<figure><img src="/assets/img/sealife-promo-3-medium-9e038d.webp" alt="Sealife promo 3 medium" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Single and album release dates for Sealife to be announced soon!</figcaption></figure>

Sealife is led by **Brennan Moring**, who also sings and plays keys for Seattle psych poppers [Peyote Ugly](/blog/review-white-feather-by-peyote-ugly). Brennan began writing poetry and playing guitar at an early age, but after learning piano, he began a conquest for total instrumental control in his songwriting and storytelling. Now, with Sealife, he’s writing love songs from the perspective of the ghost that quietly waits up for you at night.

### Follow Sealife

We're proud to be working with Sealife, and are very excited to share this paranormal love saga with you... Follow Sealife for single and album release dates, music videos, and more!
