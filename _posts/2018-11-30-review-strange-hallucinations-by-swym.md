Alt-psych duo, SWYM, has released a new single in anticipation of their upcoming EP, **Strange Hallucinations**. This single blends psychedelic atmosphere with grooving melodies and succinct lyrics.

<div class="embed embed-spotify"><iframe src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/2cGBK2Eysm5XX7bO8JybVn" title="Spotify track" height="152" loading="lazy" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture"></iframe></div>

Dreamy and lush atmospheric harmonies and soundscapes get swallowed up in swirling delays. This creates a psychedelic landscape that is easily visual in the mind.

Following self released album Mirror Box, in November of 2017, SWYM has been praised for their ability to deliver a dream like journey and smart production choices.
