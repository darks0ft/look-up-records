Lazy Legs is a Portland-based duo that have perfected shoegaze grunge. Following their early 2018 EP *Tremor*, Lazy Legs have given us a raw single that exemplifies grunge psychedelic. Nosebleed carries the thick tones of the guitar and buries the audience, leaving them in a wash of compelling tension and sound.

Nosebleed gives a minimalism and borderline ambient texture to their music and gives us much anticipation on their next release. The juxtaposition between loose mellow ambience and scratchy thick guitar sweeps showcases the duo’s choice to embrace the harmony of their sound.
