**Darksoft** releases a music video for Looking Backward, off his album Cryo, out January 14, 2022. Looking Backward was filmed on Super8mm Old Orchard Beach in Maine.

*“My idea for the video for Looking Backward started with a super simple concept. It’s similar to finding a message in a bottle on a beach — but instead of a bottle, I find a guitar crashing in on the waves. Like the process of songwriting, you pick what's out there in a sea of music, give it your own spin, and throw out something new you've written back into the waters... maybe to land on someone else's beach. Looking Backward was filmed with a Super8mm camera I found at my neighbor’s garage sale. This was my first attempt at working with actual physical film.”*
