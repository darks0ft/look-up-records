**Darksoft** releases a new single **It Is What It Is.** The track is off his upcoming album, **Beigeification**, released January 13, 2023.

The music video was filmed on VHS at empty office parks around Portland, Maine.

Take a listen to **It Is What It Is** on [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/3moi8TcbKLsbRQ0424w4jC?si=4771678992cb4c1d), [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/it-is-what-it-is-single/1651058109), or wherever else it is you stream music!
