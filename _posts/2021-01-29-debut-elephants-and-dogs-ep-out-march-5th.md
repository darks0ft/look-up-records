Stoked to announce that we'll be releasing the debut EP "**You Won't Be Seeing Me Around**" by Elephants and Dogs on Friday, March 5th.

Save the date for listening to this 5-track, syrupy trip of saturated melodies and chill dream-pop arrangements from Elephants and Dogs. Press can [**contact us**](/about) for early listens.

Until then, please follow **@dogselephants** on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/dogselephants/), [Twitter](https://twitter.com/dogselephants), and/or [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/DogsElephants/), and make them feel welcome in the vast void of weirdness. 👽💚🖤
