Look Up Records has released **Good Time**, a new single by [**BANADU**](/banadu).

Published on the summer solstice 6/21/19, Good Time features Pure Mood and uses chill electro synth, 70s style disco influences, breathy vocals, and golden guitar licks to feel out a summer groove. Hit the floor, or snap along in the dark corner of the club to Good Time.

## A VISUALIZATION:

It's 9:30 PM. Friday night. You call your bae. "Get the night off bae, let's have a Good Time." You can hear the kick pumping from a mile away. Slow fade. You hit the dance floor. Drinks. Good Time. Party. The kick doesn't stop. 4 on the floor. Glistening golden guitars. Something's coming over you. Mirrored panels light the floor. You lock eyes with them from across the floor. Time bends with the band. The bass fill and punchy snare. You just want to have a good time. You funk out til the sun comes out.
