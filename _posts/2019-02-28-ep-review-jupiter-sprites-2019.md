The dreamy synth pop music of Jupiter Sprites is perfect to get lost in. Expanding their discography from their winter 2017 EP, Gummyblossom, Jupiter Sprites released their self-titled EP on February 1st 2019.

<div class="embed embed-spotify"><iframe src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/album/1yqNDzLkxBieyaaFenhAd4" title="Spotify album" height="352" loading="lazy" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture"></iframe></div>

Perfect music to daydream to, Jupiter Sprites deliver whimsically melodic tunes with a smooth groovy rhythm. Bright washes of guitar create a perfect soundscape for their haunting and memorable vocals. Jupiter Sprites have curated this EP with wonderfully dreamy sounds and filled it with rich memorable melodies that are easy to get stuck in your head.

You can enjoy the dreamscape by Jupiter Sprites on Bandcamp, Spotify, and Apple Music. Make sure you catch them with Moon Darling, Peyote Ugly, and others at **Fred Wildlife Refuge** on March 9th for **Look Up Fest: Underwater**.
