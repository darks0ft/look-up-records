This summer solstice [**BANADU**](/banadu) blesses us with a new single, **Good Time**. A wonderful way to celebrate and kick off the summer, Good Time tosses around catchy funk bass, smooth guitar licks, and a driving beat. Perfect music to bask in the sunshine, this funk infused banger is everything we wanted after such a long hiatus.

<div class="embed embed-spotify"><iframe src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/09RYZHwayVPPzIJyHxNHLJ" title="Spotify track" height="152" loading="lazy" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture"></iframe></div>

Good Time, comes highly anticipated since their last release, Dark and Stormy: December 2017. BANADU infuses a danceability into Good Time, that matches the energy that made Last Summer, Green Glow, and Dark and Stormy wonderful.

BANADU is an indie electro project from Seattle, WA. specializing in indie-electro sound washes. The melodic guitar lines fuse with the stomping rhythm that can be felt through your whole body. Make sure to throw this single into every playlist this summer.
