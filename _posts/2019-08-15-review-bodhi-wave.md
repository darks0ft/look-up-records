DIY psyche-tinged alt-pop, Sealife teams up with Veronica Topacio to release a new single to celebrate the hazy summer days. This single blends psychedelic atmosphere with lyrical pop melodies leaving an ear worm with you the rest of the day, and a sweet summer groove in your chest.

Dreamy and lush atmospheric harmonies and soundscapes get swallowed up in swirling beats. This creates a relaxing landscape that is easily listened to on repeat all day. A perfect follow up to the 2018 album [A Paler Shade of White](/catalog), Sealife has given their audience dream-like experience with every track. A true journey with exceptionally smart production choices.
