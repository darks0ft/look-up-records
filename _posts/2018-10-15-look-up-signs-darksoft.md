Look Up Records is excited to announce that we’ve added a new project to the roster! Introducing [**Darksoft**](/darksoft). With guitar driven hooks, crystalline vocal melodies, and powerful songwriting, Darksoft will haunt your very digital mainframe. Darksoft finds inspiration in the encrypted, hidden motives that infect modern society, love, and communication.

<figure><img src="/assets/img/mydoom-album-art-f90e3b.webp" alt="Mydoom Album Art" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Darksoft: A new Look Up Records project</figcaption></figure>

Evoking an alternative sound, Darksoft creates a nostalgia for 2000s sadcore. Using an Elliot Smith and Bright Eyes vocal style, crunch guitar like Staryflyer 59 or Superheaven, and an overall garagey sound reminiscent of 90s shoegaze.

<figure><img src="/assets/img/darksoft-promo-picture-medium-03ae4f.webp" alt="Darksoft promo picture medium" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Bill Darksoft, Look Up Records founder shares his own sonic visions.</figcaption></figure>

Bill Darksoft, hailing from Seattle, leads the Darksoft project. He runs Look Up Records, and has played in and helped produce many Seattle acts over the years.
