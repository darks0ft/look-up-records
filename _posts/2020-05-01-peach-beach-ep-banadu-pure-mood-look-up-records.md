Vocalist and lyricist **Pure Mood** teams up with **Banadu** for staycation singles. 🏝️

So far, they’ve put out **Peach Beach**, and **Koopa Kape**. They’re working on an EP called Peach Beach. 🏝️

All songs are named after **Mario Kart** levels, lol. 🏎️

The EP is **for sale** on [Bandcamp](https://banadu.bandcamp.com/). 💸

You can **listen** on [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/5VqwUSrk2itakvYHfcj0JS?si=Uh08Hkl1TdOr6JQnfprk3w). 🎧

## About

*Tropical beach wave thick with dreamy night drive vibes, summer nostalgia, and salty evening breezes for moody joyrides and boardwalk arcades. The Peach Beach EP is a collab by Banadu and Pure Mood of succulent dream-funk songs inspired by Mario Kart beaches. 🍑🐚*
