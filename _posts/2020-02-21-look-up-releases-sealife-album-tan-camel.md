Look Up is excited to announce the release of **Tan Camel**, the sophomore album by [Sealife](/sealife), a project led by Seattle-based Brennan Moring.

In Tan Camel, Sealife brings us a 13 song compilation of luscious melodies, honest vocal anecdotes, bluesy licks, and dark synth-filled beats.

Sunny folky songs like **Nightflower** build to more expansive arrangements like **Into the Reeds / Out of the Reeds**. The album is interspersed with fun shorts like **Camel Dad**, a passage that feels evolved from some 90s TV intro, and the eclectic **Burning the Camel’s Feet**. Keep listening for a sexy sax ballad in the mix.

There’s a lot to love in this album and we’re very happy to finally be able to share it!

**Recommend If You Like**: Kurt Vile, Mac Demarco, Animal Collective, Donovan, Homeshake, Tame Impala.
