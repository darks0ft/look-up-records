## About Look Up Fest

Look Up Fest was a community-run, artist-run, sponsor-free mini music festival that curated indie musicians from the Pacific Northwest and beyond. Every edition had its own theme, with live visuals, immersive art installations, a photo booth, a fundraiser for a specific cause, and other surprises.

Alongside artists from the label, each fest held open submissions for other bands. Over the years Look Up Fest featured Hibou, Scott Yoder, Baja Boy, La Fonda, Moon Darling, Shimmertraps, Whitney Ballen, Antonioni, SHT GHST, Jupiter Sprites, Medejin, Peyote Ugly, Sea Salt, Coral Grief, Night Hikes and many more.

Every fest ran under the [Look Up Code of Conduct](/code-of-conduct).
