## Code of Conduct

The Look Up Records events and online spaces are meant to be a welcoming, safe, and inclusive environment for everyone involved, and therefore, we kindly ask you to read through and embrace our code of conduct. All participants, whether they are bloggers, musicians, attendees, sponsors, VJs, crew, or volunteers, are required to agree to these terms and uphold these policies. By attending an event hosted by Look Up Records, you inherently accept these terms. Look Up Records will enforce the code throughout all our events and online activities.

We will not tolerate discriminatory behavior, harassment, or victimization. Look Up is committed to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone; regardless of gender identity and expression, age, sexual orientation, political affiliation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, religion, or beliefs. During events, we expect concert-goers to be respectful of each other and the performers.

If we suspect a participant may be a threat to anyone involved in our events, or has a history of discriminatory or violent behavior that we deem unsavory, we have the right to exclude them without refund and at any time we deem necessary.

If these rules are violated, you may be blocked from our online presence or rejected from our concerts without a refund. You may also be banned from future events at the discretion of the organizers.

Throughout pandemic times, all attendees and event participants are expected to follow the venue and event organizer's safety guidelines or meet state-wide mandates.

If you notice that these policies are not being followed, please contact Look Up Records directly and we will address it.

To contact us please visit our [Contact Page](/about).
