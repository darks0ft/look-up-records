## How we help artists

## our mission

Our goal is to discover great music and increase exposure for new artists. We take on a full-stack approach to accomplish this.

## What does look up do for artists signed to the label?

It depends. In the past we’ve focused on digital distribution, playlist placement, radio promotion, PR, blog reviews, CD/tape/vinyl printing, event showcases, release planning, licensing, stickers, community vibes, and more.

Below is a list of what we do with each release. Consider these the perks of working with an indie label, as opposed to self-releasing.

### Digital Distribution

We pay for digital distribution to all major streaming platforms.

### Placement

We pitch you to some Spotify playlist curators.

### Photoshoot

We fund a photoshoot, and you're totally free to use the photos.

### Events

If applicable, we organize a release show, and showcase you at Look Up Fests.

### Physical Printing

We may fund a small promotional round of CD, Tape, and/or Vinyl printing.

### Press

We submit you to blogs for reviews.

### Newsletter

We share the release with our newsletter of industry contacts and fans.

### Radio

We send the release to some online and college radio stations.

### SHOW SHARING

We share your upcoming shows.

### Website

We set up a press kit one page site for you.

### Writing

We help write bios and descriptions of each release.

### Blog Reviews

Our writers may review your music on the Look Up Blog.

### Production

Depending on the project and budget, we might fund aspects of production, like tracking, mixing, mastering.

### Licensing

Your music is considered for film & media licensing through our partnership with the SyncFloor catalog (and more partners forthcoming).

### Publishing

To maximize royalties, we register your songs and guide artists through the PRO side of things. (ASCAP, Look Up Records Seattle, 5418746)

### Partnerships

Join a growing network of creative contacts, like bloggers, musicians, DJs, bookers, videographers, photographers, event services, and other neat folks.

### $$$

We make a budget for each release. After we cover upfront costs, we split proceeds from digital distribution, licensing, royalties, and sales with the artist.

### Support

Whether it's emotional support, exchanging ideas, or listening to demos... it really helps to have someone believing in you.

## CONSTANT EVOLUTION

The music industry is always evolving, as is the role of a record label. We want to offer a helpful service to help our artists expand that is within our means.
